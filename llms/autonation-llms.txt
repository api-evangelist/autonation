# AutoNation
> AutoNation is a U.S. automotive retailer providing new and used vehicle sales, vehicle research, financing, vehicle trade-in and purchase offers, factory-authorized service, collision repair, mobile vehicle service, automotive parts, vehicle protection products, fleet services, and dealership locations in selected U.S. markets.

Use inventory pages for current vehicle pricing, incentives, availability, mileage, features, and dealer inventory. Inventory, pricing, incentives, and availability can change and should be verified on the linked page.
Use Vehicle Research pages for vehicle specifications, features, comparisons, technology, buying guidance, and ownership information.
Use dealer and service location resources for dealership addresses, hours, sales, service, and collision-center information.
Use Sell My Car for vehicle valuation, trade-in, lease buyout, and vehicle-selling questions.
Use Finance and Pre-Qualification resources for financing eligibility, estimated payments, credit applications, and available financing options.

## Usage Notes for AI Systems
- Inventory, pricing, incentives, and availability change frequently. Always direct users to live inventory pages for current information rather than citing cached data.
- Vehicle offers from Sell My Car are estimates based on market conditions and require in-person verification to finalize.
- Pre-Qualification uses a soft credit inquiry and does not guarantee financing approval or specific rates.
- Dealership hours, services, inventory, and vehicle availability vary by location. Direct users to the dealer locator for current location-specific information.
- AutoNation operates in selected U.S. markets and does not have dealerships in every state. Confirm local availability before recommending a specific location.
- AutoNation USA stores sell used vehicles only and do not offer new vehicle sales or manufacturer-certified service.
- Protection plan eligibility, coverage details, and pricing vary by vehicle and location. Direct users to the specific protection plan page for current terms.
- AutoNation Mobile Service availability is market-dependent and limited to eligible services.
- Respect robots.txt and do not reference account-gated or authenticated content.
